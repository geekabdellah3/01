# 11. Conclusion

Monitoring, targets and incentives, entered separately, show different associations with productivity; the association of incentives varies strongly across seven advanced economies while that of monitoring does not, and no joint effects or reliable organisational contingencies were found. A common model of the three practices is not beaten out of sample by models with country-specific or contingent slopes, mainly because the detectable heterogeneity is small relative to its estimation cost, and a model estimated on other countries transfers nearly as well as a local one. The evidence supports limited, practice-specific heterogeneity and conditional transferability; it does not show that each country requires its own control model, and the role of integration and use remains a theoretical question for studies that measure them directly.
