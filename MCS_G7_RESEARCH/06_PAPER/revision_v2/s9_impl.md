# 9. Managerial and policy implications

Implications are cautious. Managers adopting practices inspired by other settings should not infer from adoption that the practices are integrated into decisions; they should assess ownership, fit with decision processes and incentives. Policy programmes that promote management practices should monitor integration and use, not only adoption. Because associations are cross-sectional and heterogeneous, the results do not support promoting a single set of practices as universally effective.
