# 4. Data and measurement

**Sample.** The merged WBES file contains 10,968 establishments from Canada (2024), France (2025), Germany (2025), Italy (2024), Japan (2025), the United Kingdom (2024) and the United States (2024). The original country files with value labels were not available. The management-practices module is asked of 6,316 establishments (88.5% with 20 or more permanent full-time employees); the others are outside the module's universe (Table 1).

**Practices.** Monitoring is whether the establishment tracked production or service performance indicators (r2, yes = 1); Targets is whether it had production or service targets (r4); Incentives is whether bonuses were tied to production or service targets (r8). Their direction (yes = 1) is verified by the skip logic of the data (follow-up items are asked only after a yes). The wording of r2 and r8 is partly verified through secondary descriptions; the wording of r4 is inferred from its follow-up item r5, whose label (time frame of production or service targets) is verified for Italy 2024. As refinements, monitoring breadth is the number of indicators monitored in three bands (r3, asked only if r2 = yes). Items r1, r6, r7, r9, r10 and r11 are not used in the main models; r10 and r11, whose wording could not be verified, enter only the complementary latent analyses (Section 6.9), with a sensitivity analysis that excludes them. The practices are single binary indicators: there is no latent measurement model, and cross-country differences in reporting cannot be tested for invariance.

**Outcomes and controls.** Outcomes are log sales per employee, annualised three-year sales growth and annualised three-year employment growth (the definitions of the base-year variables are assumed, not verified). Sales are in local currency, so levels are compared only within country. Controls are log employment, log age and sector fixed effects (sector codes are country-specific, so sector-by-country effects absorb country levels). Organisational moderators are foreign ownership of at least 10% (b2b), exporter status (d3b, d3c), top-manager experience (b7) and female top manager (b7a); their labels were checked only against data-dictionary records of other 2024 surveys.

**Survey design.** Sampling weights exist but the module universe is a subset of the sampled establishments, and stratum and cluster identifiers are not in the file; main analyses are unweighted with heteroskedasticity-robust (HC3) and sector-clustered standard errors.
