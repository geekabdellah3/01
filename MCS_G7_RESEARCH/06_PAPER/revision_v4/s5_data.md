# 5. Data and measurement

**Sample.** The study uses a merged file of WBES microdata for Canada (2024), France (2025), Germany (2025), Italy (2024), Japan (2025), the United Kingdom (2024) and the United States (2024), with 10,968 establishments. The original country files with value labels were not available; the merged file contains codes only. The management-practices module is asked of 6,316 establishments (88.5% with 20 or more permanent full-time employees); the remaining establishments are outside the module's universe, not missing responses. Table 3 gives the sample by country.

**Management-practice items.** Eleven items (r1–r11) cover monitoring, targets, bonuses and two further items. Skip patterns were verified empirically: r3 is asked only if r2 is yes, r5–r7 only if r4 is yes, r9 only if r8 is yes. Question wording was verified only partially (r5 for Italy 2024; r1–r3 and r8–r9 through secondary descriptions; r4 inferred); wording and category order of r6, r7, r10 and r11 remain **unverified**, so categories are treated as nominal and no substantive label is assigned to these items. The main specification uses six items asked of all firms in the module (r1, r2, r4, r8, r10, r11); "don't know" and "does not apply" codes are treated as missing, with full-information likelihood.

**Outcomes and controls.** Outcomes are log sales per employee, annualised three-year sales growth and annualised three-year employment growth. The definitions of the base-year variables (n3, l2) are assumed to be sales and employment three fiscal years earlier and are unverified. Sales are in local currency, so levels are compared only within country. Controls are log employment, log age and sector fixed effects; sector codes are country-specific. Firm-level moderators are foreign ownership of at least 10% (b2b), exporter status (d3b, d3c), top-manager experience (b7) and female top manager (b7a); their labels were checked only against data-dictionary records of other 2024 surveys.

**Survey design.** Sampling weights are available but the module universe is a subset of the sampled establishments; stratum and cluster identifiers are not in the file. Main analyses are unweighted, with weighted sensitivity checks, and robust or sector-clustered standard errors.
