# 3. Research questions and hypotheses

**Central question.** To what extent are the structure and performance associations of management practices universal across advanced economies, and to what extent are they contingent on national and organisational contexts?

The hypotheses were derived from the general frameworks of Section 2 after an earlier exploratory phase on the same data and were not pre-registered. Each was retained only if it could be operationalised.

- **H1 (universal technology: structure).** The observed practices share a common underlying dimension across the seven economies. *Tested by factor structure, discrete-versus-continuous model comparison and country-specific solutions.*
- **H2 (measurement).** The measurement structure differs across countries. *Tested by multi-group invariance and item differential functioning.*
- **H3 (universal technology versus contingency: effectiveness).** The association between the score and performance varies across countries. *Tested by country-specific slopes and formal tests of equality.*
- **H4 (contingency and capabilities).** The association depends on organisational contingencies (size, age, foreign ownership, exports, manager experience and gender) and on combinations of practices. *Tested by moderators, gate-combination equality at equal core scores and class-by-country interactions; national institutional moderation is not testable with seven countries and is a theoretical proposition.*
- **H5 (institutional decoupling versus artefact).** The association is weaker where adoption is high and flattens at high levels of the score. *Tested by level-dependent (piecewise) shapes, common-support analyses and below/above-median slopes; decoupling cannot be separated from ceiling or measurement error without a measure of integration.*
- **H6 (equifinality).** Different combinations of practices with equal core scores are associated with comparable performance. *Weakly testable; cross-country equifinality is a proposition.*
