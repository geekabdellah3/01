# 3. Research questions and hypotheses

**Central question.** To what extent are the structure and performance associations of management control practices universal across advanced economies, and to what extent are they contingent on national and organisational contexts?

The hypotheses were formulated after the earlier exploratory phase on the same data and were not pre-registered. Each was retained only if it could be operationalised.

- **H1 (universality of structure).** The observed practices share a common underlying sophistication dimension across the seven economies. *Testable; examined by factor structure, discrete-versus-continuous model comparison and country-specific solutions.*
- **H2 (measurement).** The measurement structure differs across countries. *Testable; multi-group invariance and item differential functioning.*
- **H3 (universality of effectiveness).** The association between the sophistication score and performance varies across countries. *Testable; country-specific slopes and formal tests of equality.*
- **H4 (combinations and context).** Performance associations of monitoring, targets and incentives depend on their combination and on organisational context. *Partly testable; gate combinations at equal core-score levels, class-by-country interactions and firm-level moderators. National institutional moderation is not testable with seven countries and is a theoretical proposition.*
- **H5 (equifinality).** Different combinations of practices are associated with comparable performance. *Weakly testable within levels of the core score; cross-country equifinality is reformulated as a proposition.*
