# 7. Limitations

1. Original `.dta` files and questionnaire wording could not be accessed; wording of r6, r7, r10, r11 is unverified and category order unknown (hence nominal treatment).
2. Module universe is mostly 20+ employee firms; results do not generalise to smaller firms.
3. Cross-sectional, self-reported practices; no causal interpretation; outcome definitions (n3, l2) unverified.
4. Sector codes are country-specific; sampling designs differ; analyses are unweighted except sensitivity checks; design-based inference not implemented.
5. Measurement invariance is partial and depends on K.
6. Bootstrap likelihood-ratio tests, BCH/ML three-step estimation and R/poLCA replication were not performed. The 2-D continuous comparator uses a coarse quadrature grid.
7. Literature search was a verified selection, not a systematic review.
