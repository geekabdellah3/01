# Deliverable F — Publication assessment (candid; no guarantee)

**Originality.** Moderate. Configurations of control have been studied (Bedford & Malmi, 2015); LCA has been used on Enterprise Surveys data for a different object (Murrell, Karalashvili & Francis, 2024). Distinctive elements: a common-likelihood comparison of discrete, continuous and hybrid structures on practice-level WBES items; explicit handling of skip patterns; a cross-country test of the practice–performance association with measurement uncertainty. The literature search was not systematic; novelty claims are provisional.

**Theoretical contribution.** Limited but coherent: separating universality of practices, structure and effectiveness from contextual fit; separating accumulation, configuration and integration. It remains a *conceptual clarification supported by exploratory evidence*; contextual fit is not tested and cannot be with these data.

**Empirical credibility.** Strengths: large sample, reproducible code, honest handling of invariance, several robustness layers, null findings reported. Weaknesses: unverified wording (r6, r7, r10, r11 and category order), partial invariance, unweighted and non-design-based inference, local-currency outcomes with unverified growth denominators, moderate reliability of the score, cross-sectional associations, heterogeneity conclusions that depend on scoring and weighting.

**Suitability (judgement, not prediction).**
- *Journal of Management Control:* the most plausible fit for an exploratory, internationally comparative empirical paper — after wording verification and a stronger theoretical section.
- *Management Accounting Research:* possible only with a substantially developed theoretical contribution, verified measures, full-text engagement with the control-package literature, and a clear answer to why the WBES practices are management *control* rather than management practice. Desk-rejection risk is high in the current state.
- *Accounting, Organizations and Society:* unlikely in the current state; the theoretical contribution and identification would need to be much stronger, and the Simons/Malmi-Brown constructs are not measured.

**Most valuable next steps (in order).** (1) Obtain questionnaires and labelled `.dta` files; (2) read in full the core papers; (3) re-run with design variables and verified outcome definitions; (4) develop the integration/use construct for the doctoral instrument rather than adding models.
