# 11. Conclusion

Monitoring, targets and incentives, entered separately, show different associations with productivity. The association of incentives appears to vary across seven advanced economies, although this heterogeneity is statistically fragile, and no joint effects or reliable organisational contingencies were found. A common model of the three practices is not detectably improved on out of sample by country-specific or contingent slopes, mainly because the explained variance is small relative to the cost of estimating additional slopes; a model estimated on other countries is close to, but slightly worse than, a local one. The evidence supports limited, practice-specific heterogeneity and conditional transferability, with limited statistical power; it does not show that each country requires its own control model, and the role of integration and use remains a theoretical question for studies that measure them directly.
