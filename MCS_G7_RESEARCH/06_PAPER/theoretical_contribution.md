# 6. Theoretical contribution and implications for future doctoral research

**Contribution (bounded).** (i) Evidence that, at the level of WBES practice items, structure is predominantly dimensional rather than typological; (ii) a procedure for testing gradient against configuration on one likelihood scale; (iii) an explicit separation of co-occurrence, statistical interaction and complementarity; (iv) a layered model that distinguishes observed practices, statistical structure, theoretical reading and associations.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR FUTURE DOCTORAL RESEARCH
*These are research propositions, not findings of the G7 study.*
1. **Questionnaire design.** Retain separate indicators for monitoring, target setting and incentives; the single dominant dimension suggests multi-item scales with distinct, verified wording.
2. **Additional indicators.** Information use (diagnostic vs interactive), beliefs, boundaries, planning, cultural and administrative controls.
3. **AI-enabled control.** Operationalise AI use in performance measurement, forecasting and anomaly detection as additional items on the same practice scales plus use-mode items.
4. **Internal audit.** Measure audit coverage, follow-up of findings and feedback into target setting.
5. **Dynamic capabilities and readiness.** Use validated scales (Teece, 2007; Armenakis & Harris, 2002) and test measurement invariance.
6. **Data needs.** Longitudinal or panel data (e.g. before/after AI adoption) and, where possible, quasi-experimental variation.
