# 3. Research questions and hypotheses

**Central question.** Do monitoring, target setting and incentives show the same individual and joint associations with performance in seven advanced economies, and how far do models of these associations transfer across organisational and national contexts?

**Question 1 (individual associations).** Is each practice associated with performance? **Question 2 (combinations).** Do practices operate independently or jointly (statistical interaction)? **Question 3 (contexts).** Are these associations stable across organisational characteristics and countries, and do models transfer?

Hypotheses (derived from the frameworks of Section 2; formulated after an earlier exploratory phase on the same data; not pre-registered):
- **H1.** Each practice is positively associated with performance (universalist benchmark).
- **H2.** The associations of practices are not additive: pairwise interactions differ from zero.
- **H3.** The association of each practice differs across countries.
- **H4.** The associations depend on organisational contingencies (size, age, foreign ownership, exports, manager characteristics).
- **H5.** Country-specific or contingent slopes improve out-of-sample prediction over common slopes, and models transfer imperfectly between countries.
- **H6.** The results do not depend on measuring practices with latent scores or breadth variants.
Institutional moderation by national characteristics (a model with MCS × Z_c) cannot be estimated with seven countries; it is nested in a model with country-specific slopes, which therefore bounds what any such interaction could add.
